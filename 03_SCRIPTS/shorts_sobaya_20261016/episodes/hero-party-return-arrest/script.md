# The Hero Party Comes Home (and Sobaya Is Taken Away)

Run 100 — no-keyframe production. Every chapter is a MiniMax H3 **R2V** generation driven by character sheets
and dialogue audio only: no keyframes are generated, and none are attached. Generation target: Google Colab
(`/colab-video`, R2V notebook). Total 1867 frames = 77.8s across 13 chapters at 24fps, 16:9.

Story: the party that beat the Window King (窓王 / "魔王おかやまん") walks back into the royal capital under a
blue sky, congratulates itself, works out that the whole quest happened because Sobaya got drunk and went wild,
and a patrol car turns up to take him away. Nothing in this run depicts bullying, harassment or a bleak ending —
the arrest is a calm, cooperative comic beat and everyone is cheerful at the end, per `WORLD_BIBLE.md`.

**The narration and the subtitles are NOT generated by H3.** H3 renders no text at all. The opening narration
audio, the two subtitles and the on-screen VOICEVOX credit are laid over the assembled video by the Remotion
project in `remotion/` (see `remotion/README.md` and `fill_edit_manifest.py`). The human-approved chapter plan
this script is built from is `chapter_plan.md` (Japanese); every chapter there is `APPROVED`.
